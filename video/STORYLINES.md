# SWITI SPANG promo — storylines

The one thing: authentic Surinamese home-food in Almere. Order before Thursday 20:00, pick up Friday. Limited batch.
Viewer remembers: "Order by Thursday or you miss it."
All copy is verbatim Dutch from the site.

## A. Donderdag 20:00 (recommended)
- Device: a moment in someone's day. Form: object-to-object chain.
- Thursday evening. The countdown on the site's "Deze week" board is ticking. One person orders saoto and roti on the real site just in time. Hard cut to Friday 17:00: the order is warm and ready.
- First 2 s: full-frame countdown digits, "Bestellen sluit donderdag 20:00", seconds ticking. A gold "Bestelling 0" pill pulses in the corner.
- Beats: countdown hook → "Wat eten we vrijdag?" → title "Switi eten. Spang geregeld." → kies (menu card, add Saoto) → betaal (cart drawer, total) → countdown hits 20:00, "Gesloten" → Friday 17:00–20:00 pickup → review quote → end card: wordmark + "Bekijk het weekmenu".
- Look: the site's own deep brown #17120f, gold #e0ae42, Playfair Display + Manrope, real site UI blown up 1.6–2×.
- Sound: warm kaseko/Caribbean groove, ticks under the countdown, a cart "pop" on each add, a bell at the pickup.

## B. Geen grote keuken
- Device: type carries the argument. Form: cut on the beat.
- Giant kinetic Playfair lines from the "Waarom" section hit on the beat: "Geen grote keuken." "Geen massaproductie." "Geen eindeloze menukaart." Each "Geen" is struck through, then the weekmenu board assembles from the type.
- First 2 s: "Geen grote keuken." fills the frame in cream on rust, and the strike hits on beat 1.
- Beats: three "Geen" lines → "We koken zoals we thuis koken." → board builds → three dishes flash → end card.
- Look: rust, gold and green colour fields from the palette, cutting every half beat.
- Sound: an up-tempo drum-led track with hard hits on every cut.

## C. Uitverkocht
- Device: a number lands. Form: one frame that rebuilds.
- The weekmenu grid fills with nine dishes. One by one they stamp "Uitverkocht" as the week runs out, until one card is left. The cursor grabs it.
- First 2 s: the full menu grid with prices, and the first "Uitverkocht" stamp slams on Saoto.
- Beats: grid → stamps accelerate → "Als het op is, is het op." → last card ordered → "Vrijdag = SWITI SPANG DAY" → end card.
- Look: the site grid on dark brown, with a rust stamp as the one material.
- Sound: a groove with stamp thuds that speed up, then a stop and a single hit on the last card.
